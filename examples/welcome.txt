Welcome to Familiar Notepad.

A little space for plain text.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For the note you need to jot down.
The config you want to check.
The idea that won't wait for a new project.

Open a file. Make a change. Save it where it belongs.


FAMILIAR KEYS

Ctrl+N       A fresh page
Ctrl+O       Open a file
Ctrl+S       Save your changes
Ctrl+F       Find that thing you wrote
Ctrl+H       Replace it
Ctrl+G       Jump to a line


FROM YOUR TERMINAL

familiar-notepad notes.txt --line 12
familiar-notepad --read-only settings.conf
familiar-notepad --inspect notes.txt


Local files. Native controls. No account.
